# 17 — Talent Qualification Framework

**Purpose:** Define, in observable terms, what "qualified" means for a WhoIsDésir creative professional, so categorization and qualification are consistent, evidence-based, and defensible.
**Status:** `PROPOSED`.
**Scope:** Applicants and approved talent across Photography, Videography, Content & Social, and Design & Creative.
**Related:** `07_TALENT_SOURCING_AND_VETTING_PLAYBOOK.md` (how to source), `08_PORTFOLIO_AND_REEL_EVALUATION_FRAMEWORK.md` (how to score craft), `18_TALENT_MATCHING_DECISION_FRAMEWORK.md` (how to match to a project).

---

## 1. Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Categorization** | Assigning discipline, secondary skills, seniority, location, and engagement type. Descriptive. |
| **Qualification** | Deciding whether the evidence meets the minimum bar to be considered for agency work. Evaluative. |
| **Eligibility** | Whether a qualified person can be matched to a specific project (availability, compliance, conflict). Situational. |
| **Qualified talent** | A person whose verified evidence meets the minimum bar for at least one service category and who consents to be considered. |
| **Qualified with gaps** | Meets the bar but has one or more missing, weak, or unverified criteria that must be resolved before a client-facing assignment. |
| **Not yet** | Promising but below the bar; may re-apply after a defined change. |
| **Ineligible** | Cannot be considered: no consent, misrepresentation, disqualifying conduct, or legal restriction. |

**Important:** qualification is not a hiring decision. It is a gate that determines whether a person enters the pool for matching. A human still decides every assignment.

---

## 2. The four gates

Every applicant passes through four gates in order. A failure at a gate stops the process with a recorded reason.

```
Gate 1: Consent and identity
   ↓
Gate 2: Category fit
   ↓
Gate 3: Evidence of craft
   ↓
Gate 4: Reliability and compliance
   ↓
Qualified / Qualified with gaps / Not yet / Ineligible
```

### Gate 1 — Consent and identity

| Check | Pass condition | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Consent | Explicit, versioned, unbundled | Consent record with timestamp |
| Identity | Name and a working contact | Email verified or human-confirmed |
| Age/eligibility | Meets the agency's working-age policy | Human confirmation; `UNKNOWN` policy today |
| Duplicate | No active duplicate | Normalized email check |
| Representation | Profile is the person's own work | Spot check; disclosure required |

**Fail:** no consent, false identity, duplicate active record, or a legal restriction.

### Gate 2 — Category fit

| Check | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Primary discipline | One of the four service categories |
| Role clarity | Can name the role they perform (photographer, editor, designer, strategist, etc.) |
| Service overlap | Secondary skills are credible, not a list of everything |
| Geography | Can work in a location or remotely within the agency's operating model |

**Fail:** discipline outside the four categories, or no identifiable role.

### Gate 3 — Evidence of craft

| Check | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| Portfolio reachable | At least one working link or 3–5 samples |
| Role on piece | The person's contribution is stated |
| Recency | At least one piece in the last 24 months, unless the role is archival/editorial |
| Range | Evidence matches the claimed seniority |
| Rights | Samples can be reviewed; client-confidential work is described, not shared |

**Fail:** no reachable evidence, or evidence does not match the claimed role.

### Gate 4 — Reliability and compliance

| Check | Pass condition |
|---|---|
| References | Two references contactable (may be completed after qualification for junior roles) |
| Documents | Required documents identified; verification can follow |
| Communication | Responds within the process SLA |
| Conduct | No disqualifying behavior in the process |
| Availability | A real availability window or a stated notice period |

**Fail:** unreachable, misrepresentation, or a disqualifying conduct finding.

## 3. Qualification criteria by vertical

Scores use the rubric in `08_PORTFOLIO_AND_REEL_EVALUATION_FRAMEWORK.md`. The table below defines the **minimum bar**, not the full rubric.

### 3.1 Photography

| Criterion | Junior | Mid | Senior | Lead |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Portfolio pieces | 8+ | 15+ | 25+ | 30+ with a body of work |
| Role clarity | Own images | Own images | Own images + lighting/art direction | Concept + team leadership |
| Technical | Exposure, focus, color | Lighting control, retouching | Full workflow, color management | Sets standard; solves on location |
| Range | One genre | Two genres | Three genres or deep specialization | Cross-genre + client direction |
| Client evidence | Personal work | Small clients | Named clients or publications | Campaigns / retained clients |
| Reliability | References available | One verified reference | Two verified references | Two + repeat engagement |

### 3.2 Videography

| Criterion | Junior | Mid | Senior | Lead |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reel length | 30–60s | 60–90s | 90s+ with case study | Full campaign |
| Role clarity | Shooter/editor | Shooter + edit | Director/DP + edit | End-to-end ownership |
| Craft | Framing, exposure | Story, pacing, sound | Color, sound design, narrative | Concept to delivery |
| Range | One format | Two formats | Three formats | Cross-format + client strategy |
| Client evidence | Personal | Small brands | Named clients | Campaigns / broadcast |
| Reliability | References available | One verified | Two verified | Two + repeat engagement |

### 3.3 Content & Social

| Criterion | Junior | Mid | Senior | Lead |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence | Personal accounts | Client accounts | Managed accounts with results | Strategy + team leadership |
| Craft | Copy, basic visuals | Content calendar, community | Channel strategy, analytics | Full-funnel strategy |
| Results | Engagement anecdote | Metrics with baseline | Documented growth/retention | Business outcomes |
| Range | One platform | Two platforms | Three platforms | Cross-platform + paid |
| Client evidence | Personal | Small brands | Named clients | Retained clients |
| Reliability | References available | One verified | Two verified | Two + repeat engagement |

### 3.4 Design & Creative

| Criterion | Junior | Mid | Senior | Lead |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Portfolio | 6+ pieces | 12+ pieces | 20+ with systems | 25+ with brand systems |
| Craft | Layout, type basics | Type, grid, color systems | Design systems, motion | Art direction + systems |
| Range | One discipline | Two disciplines | Three disciplines | Cross-discipline + strategy |
| Process | Final artifacts | Process shown | Research to system | Research to launch |
| Client evidence | Personal | Small brands | Named clients | Campaigns / retained |
| Reliability | References available | One verified | Two verified | Two + repeat engagement |

**Seniority is assigned from evidence, not self-description.** A person who claims "senior" but presents junior evidence is categorized at the evidence level and told why.

## 4. Bands and confidence

### 4.1 Bands

| Band | Definition | Next action |
|---|---|---|
| **Qualified** | All four gates pass; evidence meets the bar | Enter the pool; eligible for matching |
| **Qualified with gaps** | Gates pass but one or more criteria are weak or unverified | Enter the pool with a gap list; resolve before client-facing assignment |
| **Not yet** | Below the bar, but a defined change could qualify | Record the gap and a re-apply date |
| **Ineligible** | Fails Gate 1, or a disqualifier applies | Close with a reason; no matching |

### 4.2 Confidence

| Confidence | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **High** | Direct evidence for every gate; two references; documents verified |
| **Medium** | Direct evidence for craft; one gate relies on a credible but unverified claim |
| **Low** | Thin evidence, self-reported seniority, or one weak gate |

**Rule:** `qualified` with low confidence may be matched only to low-risk, supervised work, and only with a human note. High-risk or client-facing work requires medium or high confidence.

---

## 5. Disqualifiers

Record the disqualifier and the evidence. Do not diagnose or speculate.

| Disqualifier | Action |
|---|---|
| No consent to store or review | Cannot process; explain and stop |
| Misrepresentation of work (claiming another's work) | Ineligible; record evidence |
| Harassment, threats, or abusive conduct in the process | Ineligible; escalate to Lead Developer |
| Fraudulent documents | Ineligible; escalate; preserve evidence |
| Legal restriction on working with minors or vulnerable people (where relevant) | Ineligible for affected work; escalate |
| Active conflict of interest with a client | Eligible for other work; exclude from the conflicted project |
| Duplicate active record | Merge under human control; do not double-count |

**Never disqualify for:** disability, pregnancy, age (within policy), nationality, religion, gender, or any protected characteristic. Accommodation requests are handled, not penalized.

## 6. Decision tree

```
Consent + identity?
├─ No → stop; explain; do not create a full profile
└─ Yes ↓
Category fit?
├─ No → "not yet" or decline with reason; do not force a category
└─ Yes ↓
Reachable evidence?
├─ No → "qualified with gaps" + request samples; re-check at 10 business days
└─ Yes ↓
Evidence meets the bar for the claimed level?
├─ No → categorize at the evidence level; "not yet" if below minimum
└─ Yes ↓
References and compliance?
├─ No → "qualified with gaps"; open verification tasks
└─ Yes → "qualified" with confidence level
│
└─ Any disqualifier? → "ineligible" + escalation
```

## 7. Worked examples

### 7.1 Photographer, strong craft, no client work

- 20 event images, clear ownership, consistent color, two personal projects.
- No named clients, one reference.
- **Qualified with gaps** at mid level. Gaps: client evidence, second reference. Eligible for supervised event work; not for a lead role.

### 7.2 Videographer, beautiful reel, unclear role

- 90-second reel, high production value, no role stated, no case study.
- **Qualified with gaps** at mid level pending role confirmation. If the person was the editor only, re-categorize to editor; do not credit direction.

### 7.3 Designer, strong craft, no systems

- 15 beautiful posters, no design system, no process.
- **Qualified with gaps** at mid level for campaign design; not for a product design system role.

### 7.4 Social strategist, results without baselines

- Claims 300% growth, no baseline, no screenshots.
- **Not yet** until evidence is provided. Do not accept the claim at face value.

### 7.5 Senior claim, junior evidence

- Claims 10 years, portfolio shows student work and one small client.
- Categorize at junior/mid based on evidence; explain the gap; offer a re-review path.

### 7.6 Disqualifying misrepresentation

- Two portfolio pieces belong to another photographer, confirmed by the original creator.
- **Ineligible**; record evidence; escalate; do not contact the client about the person.

## 8. Re-application and appeals

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| `not yet` | Re-apply after the recorded date (default 6 months) or when the specific gap is closed |
| `qualified with gaps` | Gaps can be closed at any time; no full re-application needed |
| `declined` after review | Re-apply after 12 months, or sooner if the agency invites it |
| `ineligible` | No re-application without Lead Developer review |
| Appeal | One written appeal; reviewed by a different reviewer; decision recorded |

An appeal is about process and evidence, not about pressure. The reviewer must not be the person who made the original decision.

---

## 9. Calibration

- Monthly: two reviewers independently score 10 applications; compare bands.
- Target agreement: 80% exact band, 95% within one band.
- Disagreements are discussed and the rubric or guidance is updated.
- Track band distribution by vertical, source, and reviewer; investigate outliers.
- Never calibrate by outcome alone (for example, "we need more people, so lower the bar").

---

## 10. Record requirements

Every qualification decision stores:

- applicant ID and reference,
- reviewer ID,
- rubric version and qualification framework version,
- gate results with evidence links,
- band and confidence,
- gaps and next action,
- disqualifier (if any) with evidence,
- timestamp.

`TalentReview` and `TalentDecision` are defined in `12_SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE_AND_DATA_MODEL.md`.

---

## 11. What qualification is not

- It is not a promise of work.
- It is not a rate negotiation.
- It is not a contract.
- It is not a substitute for a project-specific eligibility check.
- It is not permanent; it is re-checked when evidence or compliance changes.
