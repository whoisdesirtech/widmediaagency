Getting Started
Essential lessons for everyone — vendors, clients, and admins
1
How to Log In
Everyone ▾

Every user — vendor, client, or admin — logs in through the same portal.

1
Open the login page: https://whoisdesirmediaagency.vercel.app/login
2
Enter your email and password. These were provided to you by your agency admin.
3
Click "Sign In." You'll be automatically redirected to your portal based on your role.
Tip: Bookmark the login page for quick access. You can log in from any device.
2
Forgot Your Password?
Everyone ▾
1
Go to the login page and click "Forgot password?"
2
Enter your email address and click "Reset Password."
3
Check your email for a reset link. Follow the instructions to set a new password.
Still can't log in? Contact your agency admin directly — they can reset your password manually.
3
Understanding Your Dashboard
Everyone ▾

After logging in, you land on your Dashboard. This is your home base.

  • Vendors see: Active contracts, pending signatures, SOWs, documents needed
  • Clients see: Active projects, deliverables, invoices, media
  • Admins see: Total contractors, active SOWs, pending contracts, all contractors list

Use the sidebar on the left to navigate to different sections.

Vendor Lessons
For contractors and freelancers working with the agency
4
Uploading Onboarding Documents
Vendor ▾

Before your contract can be finalized, upload your required documents.

1
Click "Onboarding" in the sidebar → navigate to /contractor/onboarding
2
Tax Form (Required): Upload your W-9 (U.S.) or W-8BEN (international). Accepted: PDF, JPG, PNG.
3
Insurance Proof (if applicable): Required for on-location work. Upload your liability certificate.
4
Licensing Proof (if applicable): Required for specialized roles (drone, etc.).
File limit: 10MB per file. Once all required documents are uploaded, you'll see a green confirmation banner.
5
Reviewing Your Statement of Work (SOW)
Vendor ▾

Your SOW defines your rate, payment schedule, and deliverables.

1
Go to Dashboard → scroll to "My Statements of Work"
2
Review: Rate Type, Rate, Start Date, Payment Schedule, Status

SOW Statuses:

  • Draft — Still being prepared by your agency
  • Approved — Agency approved. Contract will be generated next
  • Active — In effect, work is underway
  • Completed — Engagement ended
6
Reviewing & Signing Your Contract
Vendor ▾

After your SOW is approved and documents uploaded, your agency assembles the contract.

1
Click "My Contracts" in the sidebar → /contractor/contracts
2
Select the contract to load the full preview. Read the Master Agreement, SOW terms, and role-specific addendum.
3
Scroll to "Your Signature" → draw your signature → click "Sign"
Important: This is a legally binding agreement. Read it carefully and ask your agency admin if you have questions before signing.
7
Uploading Project Deliverables
Vendor ▾
1
Go to Projects in the sidebar to see your assigned projects
2
Click into a project → upload images, documents, or files
3
Go to Deliverables to track pending, in-progress, and approved items
Tip: You can also deliver photos directly to the client's Google Drive if it's configured (see Google Drive lessons below).
8
Delivering Photos to Client's Google Drive
Vendor ▾

If your agency has linked a Google Drive folder to the client, you can upload photos directly to their Drive.

1
Open the project → click "Deliver Photos to Client Drive"
2
Select photos from your device (JPG, JPEG, PNG — max 15MB each)
3
Files upload directly to the client's shared Google Drive folder
Don't see the option? Your agency admin hasn't linked a Google Drive folder to this client yet. Ask them to set it up.

Google Drive Integration
How vendor uploads and client media galleries work
14
How Google Drive Integration Works
Everyone ▾

The platform uses Google Drive to store and share project files between vendors, clients, and admins.

  • Admins create a Shared Drive and link client folders to the platform
  • Vendors upload photos directly to the client's Drive folder
  • Clients browse their Media Gallery to view uploaded files
Important: Client folders must be in a Shared Drive (not My Drive). Service accounts cannot upload to personal Drive folders.
15
Troubleshooting Drive Uploads
Vendor ▾

If you see errors when uploading to Google Drive:

  • "Service Accounts do not have storage quota" — The folder is in My Drive, not a Shared Drive. Ask your admin to move it.
  • 403 / Permission error — The Shared Drive hasn't been shared with the service account. Ask your admin to add it.
  • 503 error — Google Drive is temporarily unavailable. Try again later.
  • Option doesn't appear — Your admin hasn't linked a Google Drive folder to this client yet.

Frequently Asked Questions
Common questions and quick answers
16
I can't log in — what should I do?
Everyone ▾
  1. Use the "Forgot password?" link on the login page
  2. Check your email (including spam/junk folder)
  3. If that doesn't work, contact your agency admin — they can reset your password manually
17
My file upload failed — what do I do?
Everyone ▾
  • Check file size: Onboarding docs max 10MB, project images max 15MB
  • Check file type: Onboarding accepts PDF, JPG, PNG. Project images accept JPG, JPEG, PNG, WEBP, GIF
  • Check your connection: Try again with a stable internet connection
  • Google Drive errors: See the Google Drive troubleshooting lesson above
18
How do I get a copy of my signed contract?
Everyone ▾

Go to "My Contracts" (vendors) or "Documents" (clients) → select the contract → click "Download" to save an HTML copy.

19
Who do I contact for help?
Everyone ▾
  • Login issues: Your agency admin can reset your password
  • Contract questions: Ask your agency admin before signing
  • Technical issues: Contact your agency support team directly
  • Billing questions: Reach out to your agency's finance contact
