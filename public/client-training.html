1 Logging In

Your agency admin will provide you with login credentials. Here's how to access your portal:

1

Open the login page in your browser

https://widmediaagency.web.app/login
2

Enter your email and password

Use the credentials your agency admin sent you. These are the same email and password they set up when creating your account.

3

Click "Sign In"

You'll be redirected to your Client Dashboard.

Tip: Bookmark the login page for easy access next time. You can also use the "Forgot password?" link if you need to reset your password.
Keep Your Credentials Safe: Never share your login credentials publicly. If you suspect your account has been compromised, contact your agency admin immediately.

3 Accessing Your Media (Google Drive)

The Media Gallery is where you can view, browse, and download all your project files. Your agency uses Google Drive to store and share files with you.

1

Click "Media Gallery" in the sidebar

Or click "View Media Gallery" from your dashboard quick actions.

2

Browse your folders

You'll see folders your agency created (e.g., "Photography", "Video", "Final Deliverables"). Click a folder to open it and see the files inside.

3

Preview files

Your Google Drive folder is embedded directly in the page. Click any image to preview it full-size, or click a video to play it — all without leaving the portal.

4

Download files

To download, click the "Open in Google Drive" button at the top of the gallery. This opens the folder directly in Google Drive in a new tab, where you can download individual files or entire folders to your device.

5

New files appear automatically

When vendors upload photos or videos to your Drive folder, they appear in your Media Gallery automatically — no refresh needed. Just open the folder to see the latest files.

💡 Tips
On mobile, you may need the Google Drive app for the best viewing experience.
You don't need a Google account to browse the embedded gallery — it works without signing in.
To download multiple files at once, use the "Open in Google Drive" button and select files there.
⚠ Gallery empty?
Your agency admin may not have linked a Google Drive folder to your account yet. Ask them to set it up. Once linked, any files vendors upload will appear here automatically.

5 Troubleshooting & FAQ

Common Issues

!

"No Client Profile" message

This means your account isn't linked to a client profile yet. Contact your agency admin to complete the setup.

!

"No Media Folder Configured"

Your admin hasn't linked a Google Drive folder to your account yet. Ask them to add your media folder ID in the client settings.

!

Media folder shows "Access Denied"

The Google Drive folder may not be shared with your account. Ask your agency admin to ensure the folder is shared with "Anyone with the link" or with your specific Google account.

!

Can't log in / "Invalid credentials"

Double-check your email and password. Use the "Forgot password?" link to reset. If that doesn't work, contact your agency admin.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q

Can I upload files to my folder?

Currently, the client portal is view/download only. If you need to upload files, contact your agency admin directly.

Q

How do I get new media added to my folder?

Your agency admin uploads new media to your Google Drive folder. Once added, it appears automatically in your Media Gallery.

Q

Can I share my login with my team?

Your login is for your personal use. If team members need access, ask your agency admin to create additional client accounts.

Q

Do I need a Google account?

No. The media gallery is embedded in the portal, so you can browse and download without signing into Google. However, you may need a Google account to use the full Google Drive features if you click "Open in Google Drive."
