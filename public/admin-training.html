2 Adding a New Contractor

1

Navigate to Contractors → Click "+ Add Contractor"

Or go to /contractors/new

2

Fill in the contractor details

Name: Their full name or business name
Business Name: LLC or DBA name
Role: Select from dropdown (Photography, Videography, Social Media, Designer, AI Automation, Web Designer, Developer, Copywriter, Motion Designer, Virtual Assistant, Marketing Specialist, Podcast Editor)
State: Their operating state
Country: Defaults to United States

3

Click "Create Contractor"

You'll be taken to their detail page where you can manage everything.

Tip: The role you select determines which legal addendum is automatically attached to their contract.

3 Generating Contractor Login

Create login credentials so vendors can access their own portal.

1

Go to the Contractor's detail page

Click on their name from the Dashboard or Contractors list

2

In "Quick Actions" — enter their email

Type their real email address in the Contractor Email field

3

Click "Generate Contractor Login"

The system creates their account and shows a temporary password. Copy and send these to them securely.

Important: Share credentials privately (email, text, etc.). Never post them publicly.

4 Creating SOW & Assembling Contracts

After adding a contractor, create their Statement of Work, then assemble the final contract.

SOW (Statement of Work)

1

Go to SOW Builder (/sow-builder)

Fill in rate type, rate, payment schedule, start/end dates, deliverables

2

Approve the SOW from the contractor's detail page

The status changes from Draft → Approved. Contracts cannot be assembled until the SOW is approved.

Assemble & Sign

3

Go to Assembled Contracts (/contracts)

Select the contractor, click "Assemble Contract". The system merges Master Agreement + Addendum + SOW.

4

Draw your signature as the agency representative

Then the contractor signs from their portal. Once both sign, the contract becomes Active.

5 Managing Clients & Projects

The platform tracks clients, their projects, deliverables, and invoices.

1

Add clients from /clients

Each client gets a portal with their projects, documents, and media gallery.

2

Create projects under /admin/projects

Assign contractors, track timelines, manage deliverables and upload project images.

Tip: Clients can log in at /login with their credentials. They see only their own projects, documents, and invoices.

6 Linking Google Drive to Clients

Vendors can upload photos directly to a client's Google Drive folder. To enable this, you need to link a Drive folder to each client.

Step 1: Create a Shared Drive

Service accounts cannot upload to personal "My Drive" folders. You must use a Shared Drive.

1

Open Google Drive with your agency account

Go to drive.google.com and sign in with the agency Google account

2

Create a Shared Drive

Click Shared drives in the left sidebar → + New → name it (e.g. "WhoIsDésir Media") → Create

3

Share the Shared Drive with the service account

Open the shared drive → Share → add widmedia-drive-upload@whoisdesir-media.iam.gserviceaccount.com as Content manager → Send

Step 2: Create Client Folders

4

Create a folder for each client inside the Shared Drive

Navigate into the Shared Drive → New folder → name it after the client (e.g. "Start Social"). If the folder already exists in My Drive, right-click → Organize → Move → pick the Shared Drive.

5

Copy the folder ID from the URL

Open the client folder. The URL looks like:
https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1aBcDeFgHiJkLmNoPqRsT
Copy the bold part — that's the folder ID.

Step 3: Link in the Admin Portal

6

Go to Clients → click the client → edit their profile

Paste the folder ID into the Google Drive Folder ID field. Optionally add the full URL in Google Drive Folder URL. Click Save.

Tip: Once linked, vendors can use "Deliver Photos to Client Drive" in their portal to upload directly to this folder.
Important: If you see "Service Accounts do not have storage quota" errors, the folder is in My Drive instead of a Shared Drive. Move it to the Shared Drive.

7 Admin Quick-Start Checklist

  • Log in and review the Dashboard
  • Review the 20 Master Agreement clauses with a licensed attorney
  • Add your first contractor and assign a role
  • Generate their login credentials and share privately
  • Create a Statement of Work and approve it
  • Assemble the contract and sign as agency representative
  • Add clients and create their first project
  • Review Settings — agency name, jurisdiction, communication tools
Legal Disclaimer: This system generates draft contracts. All agreements must be reviewed by a licensed attorney before execution.
