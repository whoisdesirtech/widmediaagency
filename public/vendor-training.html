3 Viewing Your Statement of Work (SOW)

Your SOW defines your rate, payment schedule, and deliverables.

1

Go to your Dashboard

Click Dashboard in the left sidebar

2

Scroll to "My Statements of Work"

You'll see your SOWs with rate type, rate, start date, payment schedule, and status.

SOW Statuses
Draft — Your agency admin is still preparing this. Not final yet.
Approved — The agency has approved. Your contract will be generated next.
Active — The SOW is in effect and work is underway.
Completed — The engagement has ended.

4 Uploading Your Documents

Before your contract can be finalized, upload the required documentation.

1

Click "Onboarding" in the sidebar

Navigate to /contractor/onboarding

2

Upload your Tax Form (Required)

Select W-9 (U.S. residents) or W-8BEN (international). Accepted formats: PDF, JPG, PNG.

3

Upload Insurance Proof (if applicable)

Required if you do on-location work. Upload your general liability certificate.

4

Upload Licensing Proof (if applicable)

Required for specialized work (drone photography, etc.).

Tip: File sizes are capped at 10MB per upload. Once all required documents are uploaded, you'll see a green confirmation banner.

5 Reviewing & Signing Your Contract

After your SOW is approved and documents are uploaded, your agency assembles the contract.

1

Click "My Contracts" in the sidebar

Navigate to /contractor/contracts

2

Select the contract and review it

The contract contains: Master Agreement, your information, SOW terms, and role-specific addendum.

Important: Read the entire contract carefully before signing. This is a legally binding agreement. Ask your agency admin if you have questions.
3

Draw your signature

Scroll to "Your Signature" at the bottom. Draw with your mouse or finger. Click "Sign" when done.

4

Done!

Once both you and the agency have signed, the contract status changes to "Active".

6 Projects & Deliverables

Once assigned to a project, you can track your work and upload deliverables.

1

View assigned projects

Go to Projects in the sidebar. Each project shows timeline, progress, and deliverables.

2

Upload deliverables

Click into a project to upload images, documents, or other files. You can also deliver photos directly to the client's Google Drive (if configured).

3

Track deliverable status

Go to Deliverables to see pending, in-progress, and approved items.

7 Your Onboarding Checklist

Complete these steps to get started:

  • Log in to the contractor portal
  • Upload your W-9 (or W-8BEN if international)
  • Upload insurance proof (if doing on-location work)
  • Upload licensing proof (if applicable)
  • Review your Statement of Work on the dashboard
  • Wait for your agency to approve the SOW
  • Review the assembled contract in "My Contracts"
  • Sign the contract
  • Download a copy for your records
  • Start working on assigned projects

8 Need Help?

If you have any issues with the portal or questions about your agreement:

  • Login issues? Contact your agency admin to reset your password
  • Contract questions? Ask your agency admin before signing
  • Document upload issues? Make sure files are PDF, JPG, or PNG and under 10MB
  • General questions? Reach out to your agency contact directly
